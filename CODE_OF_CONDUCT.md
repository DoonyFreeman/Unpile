# Community conduct

Be respectful and specific. Discuss ideas and behavior without harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, or pressure to share private information.

Help contributors reproduce problems with synthetic files. Respect other people's time and language preferences. Never request screenshots of real Downloads or disclose someone else's personal data.

For security or file-safety reports use the private route described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For sensitive community reports, request a private contact from the maintainer without posting the details publicly. Maintainers may remove harmful material and restrict participation; explain routine moderation decisions without republishing private details.

Maintainer: [DoonyFreeman](https://github.com/DoonyFreeman). This policy does not authorize contacting anyone on the user's behalf.
